About Aroma Coffee & Tea House

Established in 2015, Aroma Coffee & Tea House was founded by two coffee enthusiasts who wanted to create a warm, inviting space where people could enjoy high-quality, ethically sourced coffee and tea. The idea was born after a trip to Kenya's tea farms and Colombian coffee plantations, where we witnessed the passion of local farmers. We opened our first shop in Nairobi, and later expanded online to share these incredible flavours with the world.

Our Mission: To provide our customers with the finest coffee and tea while supporting sustainable farming and fair trade. We believe that every cup should tell a story of quality, care, and community.

Our Vision: To become a global hub for coffee and tea lovers, known for exceptional products, ethical sourcing, and a welcoming experience that brings people together.
